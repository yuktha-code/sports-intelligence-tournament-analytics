This was a NextLeap team analytics exercise using sports datasets involving players, teams and transfers.

## Analytical work
- Filters, joins, grouping, conditional logic and ranking.
- Table-grain and join-cardinality checks before combining data.
- Origin/destination team joins with aliases.
- Value-for-money and budget-efficiency ratios with explicit denominator rules.
- Careful interpretation of rankings without turning descriptive ratios into causal claims.

## Key lesson
A syntactically correct query can still be analytically wrong if the grain, join cardinality or denominator is wrong.
